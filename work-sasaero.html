About PT SAS Aero Sishan

PT SAS Aero Sishan is a defense and aerospace engineering company based in Bandung, focused on developing advanced defense systems, aerospace technology, automation, fabrication, precision machining, and unmanned aerial vehicle solutions.

What I did

  • Completed a Systems Division internship on MATS (Moving Armor Target System), a defence prototype collaboration involving PT SAS Aero Sishan, Telkom University and Pussenkav TNI AD; the project underwent testing and demonstration for the military.
  • Designed the Pixhawk 6C ROV wiring architecture, considering component voltages, signal flow and port allocation; reviewed designs in weekly technical meetings with partners.
  • Modified the telemetry interface using Qt Creator and QGroundControl, supported by Visual Studio, to add front/rear camera feeds to an ROV interface mockup.
  • Designed 3D-printable prototype casings and electronics enclosures in Autodesk Inventor for the Pixhawk 6C, batteries and other components; revised designs following team reviews.
  • Used a Waterfall/V-Model approach and delivered ROV power-distribution schematics and a GCS mockup presented to the client as a basis for fabrication.
