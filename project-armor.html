About the Moving Armor Target System (MATS)

MATS is a defence prototype collaboration involving PT SAS Aero Sishan, Telkom University and Pussenkav TNI AD: a moving target based on a tracked robot vehicle, designed for military live-fire training to simulate a dynamic target on the shooting range.

What I did

  • Designed a Pixhawk 6C electrical block architecture and power-distribution documentation considering component voltages, signal paths and port allocation; planned GPS and front/rear camera telemetry.
  • Modified the Qt Creator/QGroundControl interface to add camera feeds.
  • Designed casings and electronics enclosures in Autodesk Inventor, balancing protection and access for the electronics with the structural needs of field conditions.
  • Produced technical schematics and interface mockups for subsequent fabrication.

Field testing

The system underwent testing and demonstration on the shooting range, witnessed by the Indonesian Armed Forces (TNI) and the related team, to validate the moving target's performance in a training scenario.
