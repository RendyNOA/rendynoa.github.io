About PUI-PT Intelligent Sensing-IoT

PUI-PT Intelligent Sensing-IoT (IS-IoT) is a Telkom University research center focused on Intelligent Sensing, the Internet of Things (IoT), Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN), and Artificial Intelligence (AI). The center develops innovative sensing technologies for smart, connected, and sustainable solutions through collaboration with academia and industry.

What I did

  • Completed a semester-long research internship supporting two PUI-PT IS-IoT products: TrapGradien (ioniser-based air filtration) and AirGradien (wearable air-quality monitoring).
  • Selected/procured sensors, assembled and tested sensor–microcontroller connections, and built/improved a calibration chamber for system evaluation.
  • Disassembled an AirGradien unit to examine sensor positioning and airflow; studied chamber MFC operation, then reassembled and integrated sensors, battery power and website connectivity.
  • Installed multiplexers for parallel calibration of matching sensor types and prepared a 3D printer for TrapGradien and AirGradien enclosures/fixtures.
  • Prepared a final report documenting sensor selection through integration, reviewed and approved by academic and PUI-PT IS-IoT field supervisors.
